About Castello

We built Castello because creators deserve to own what they create — without spending their time chasing down theft.

Every day, thousands of creators have their content stolen, reposted, and monetised by others. Leaked content costs creators real revenue, damages their reputation, and can feel deeply violating. Most creators don't have the time, technical knowledge, or legal resources to fight back.

That's why Castello exists. We combine AI-powered scanning with rapid DMCA enforcement to find and remove stolen content across the internet — automatically, around the clock, and without exposing your identity.

What we believe

Creators come first

Everything we build starts with the question: does this make a creator's life easier? If it doesn't, we don't ship it.

Privacy is non-negotiable

Your identity is never shared with infringers. We handle every takedown on your behalf so you stay protected.

Speed matters

Stolen content spreads fast. Our average takedown time is under 60 minutes because every minute counts.

Transparency always

Your dashboard shows exactly what we've found and removed. No black boxes, no hidden processes.

Our reach

Castello monitors over 75 million websites, including piracy sites, forums, Telegram groups, social media platforms, and file-sharing services. We've completed over 500,000 takedowns for more than 2,000 creators across 150+ countries.

Whether you're just starting out or you're a top creator, Castello scales with you. Our AI gets smarter the more it works, and our team of DMCA specialists handles the cases that need a human touch.
